2. Summary

  • This website sets no cookies and uses no local storage.
  • We do not use advertising, social media plugins, or cross-site tracking.

3. Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA.

Whenever you open a page, your browser automatically sends technical data to GitHub's servers: your IP address, the date and time of the request, the page requested, the referring page, and information about your browser and operating system. GitHub processes this data to deliver the website and to keep the service secure and stable. We have no access to these logs. GitHub determines how long it retains them; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in providing a reliable and secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Transfer to the USA: GitHub, Inc. is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Transfers rely on the European Commission's adequacy decision of 10 July 2023 (Art. 45 GDPR).

More information: GitHub General Privacy Statement.

4. Website analytics

We use Plausible Analytics, provided by Plausible Insights OÜ, Västriku tn 2, 50403 Tartu, Estonia, to understand how our website is used (e.g. page views, referring websites, country, device type). Plausible sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device. Your IP address and browser information are combined with a salt that changes daily to create an anonymous identifier; the IP address itself is not stored, and the salt is deleted after 24 hours. All data is processed on servers in the EU and only as aggregated statistics. Plausible acts as our processor.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in understanding and improving the use of our website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can object at any time (see "Your rights"), for example by using a browser extension that blocks the script.

More information: Plausible Data Policy.

5. Contact form and email

When you use our contact form, we process the name, email address and message you enter. Formspree also receives your IP address and technical information about your browser when you submit the form. When you email us, we process your email address and the content of your message.

Our contact form is operated by Formspree, Inc., USA. Formspree receives your submission, stores it on servers in the USA, and forwards it to us by email. Formspree acts as our processor. Transfers to the USA are protected by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR).

Our email is hosted by OVH SAS, 2 rue Kellermann, 59100 Roubaix, France, which processes emails sent to us on our behalf. OVH acts as our processor.

Purpose: to reply to your inquiry and, where relevant, to prepare a possible engagement.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if your inquiry relates to a possible contract with us; otherwise our legitimate interest in replying to messages addressed to us (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Retention: we delete your inquiry once it has been fully dealt with. We delete submissions stored at Formspree no later than six months after receipt. If your inquiry leads to a business relationship, statutory retention periods under German commercial and tax law apply (up to ten years, § 257 HGB, § 147 AO).

This website links to external websites such as LinkedIn, GitHub, Google Patents, IEEE, NIST and the Proceedings on Privacy Enhancing Technologies. These are plain links: no data is sent to these websites unless you click one. Once you do, the privacy policy of that website applies.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • have inaccurate data corrected (Art. 16 GDPR),
  • have your data deleted (Art. 17 GDPR),
  • have the processing of your data restricted (Art. 18 GDPR),
  • receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format (Art. 20 GDPR).

To exercise any of these rights, email contact@modalresonance.com.

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR). Where we process your data on the basis of our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), you may object to this processing at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. We will then stop processing your data unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, rights and freedoms, or the processing serves the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.
